We Give A FAQ

An AI Decision Intelligence OS is purpose-built infrastructure for strategic and capital decision-making. Unlike general chatbots that output open-ended conversational text, Offc operates machine-natively: it ingests verified accounting and operational data, evaluates mathematical risk distributions, and produces deterministic, binding action plans.
Instead of asking "Is this a good idea?", Offc uses the cognitive psychological technique of pre-mortems. It simulates that your initiative has failed disastrously 6 to 12 months into the future. It then works backward to expose the hidden vulnerabilities—demand drops, cash crunches, supplier lead-time blowouts—and pairs each failure mode with a preventive antidote.
Offc connects securely to your ERP, CRM, and accounting systems via encrypted read-only API connectors. Data is parsed into structured telemetry matrices. The models are not allowed to guess or hallucinate numbers; every assumption must be cross-checked against your real bank buffers, run rates, and supplier contracts.
The AI Board is an ensemble of specialized domain models—including a CEO Orchestrator, CFO, Chief Risk Officer, Sales Strategist, and Operations Director. When you submit a major decision, the agents debate adversarial trade-offs from their respective perspectives until balanced executive consensus is reached.
